Style Shift Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Style Shift ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Style Shift does not require account registration. The app uses the following information only to provide its image generation features:

  • Photos: Images you take or select as references for hairstyle, hair color, and fashion generation.
  • App preferences & Identifiers: Language, approximate location (country), onboarding progress, premium status, unique device identifiers (UUID), and advertising identifiers (AD_ID) stored on your device.
  • Optional feedback: If you send feedback, we receive the message you write, your app version, and an app-specific identifier.

2. How We Use Your Photos

Photos you submit are sent to our server solely to create AI-generated style transformations. We do not use your photos for unrelated purposes.

3. Local Data Storage

Generation history, app preferences, premium status, and review-related state are stored locally on your device. This information is removed when you uninstall the application.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements and personalize ad content.
  • Google Play: Used for in-app purchases and review prompts.
  • Google Firebase: Used for app analytics, crash reporting, and performance monitoring.
  • AI processing providers: Used to generate style transformation images from your submitted references.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal data. We only share information as needed to provide image generation, advertising, purchases, review prompts, and optional feedback handling.

6. Children's Privacy

Style Shift is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Style Disclaimer

Generated images are AI-created visual previews for inspiration and entertainment. Results may vary based on lighting, pose, image quality, and selected options.

8. Security

Photos and generation requests are transmitted over a secure connection. If you send feedback, the message, app version, and app-specific identifier may be stored on our servers to review reports and reduce abuse. Photos submitted for processing are kept temporarily on our servers only for the duration of the generation process and subsequent history availability.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
